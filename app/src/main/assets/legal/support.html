Block Balance · version 1.0.0

How a run works

Pick a risk tier, set a stake and press the stack button. The first block is released right away. Every block that stays on the stack multiplies your stake. Press bank at any time to take the payout; if a block misses, the stake is lost and the run ends.

Frequently asked

Can I time the hoist to make a block land?

No. The result of each release is decided by the risk tier you picked, not by where the hoist happens to be. The swing is there for the feel of it.

What does the risk tier change?

A higher tier lowers the chance that a block holds, widens the multiplier range and speeds the hoist up. Steady is the safest, Chaos the wildest.

Where do credits come from?

Banked runs, the daily reward, daily quests and rank rewards. Credits are game points only: they cannot be purchased and have no cash value.

I lost my progress

Progress lives in the app's private storage on the device. Clearing app data, uninstalling or using Reset progress wipes it, and it cannot be recovered because nothing is stored on a server.

Are the weekly ranks real players?

No. The table is generated on your device for the current week. Only your own score is real.

The game feels slow or stutters

Close background apps and make sure battery saver is off, since it can cap the frame rate. Restarting the game rebuilds the art cache.

Still stuck?

Write to the developer contact address on the Google Play store listing for Block Balance. Please include your device model and Android version.
