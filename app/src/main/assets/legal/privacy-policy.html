Privacy Policy

Block Balance · last updated 14 August 2026

Block Balance is a single player casual game. It is built so that playing it requires no account, no sign-in and no personal data.

What the game stores

Your progress is written to private storage on your own device and never leaves it. That record contains only game values:

  • in-game credit balance and experience
  • owned and equipped cosmetic items
  • selected risk tier and last used stake
  • daily reward streak and daily quest state
  • personal best height, payout and streak

Uninstalling the game, or using Reset progress in Settings, removes that record.

What the game does not do

  • no advertising and no ad identifiers
  • no analytics or crash reporting services
  • no purchases and no payment processing
  • no access to contacts, photos, location or microphone
  • no accounts, no profiles, no cloud sync

Weekly ranks

The weekly rank table is generated on your device. The rival entries are computer generated for the current week and are not other players. No score is uploaded anywhere.

Network use

The game itself runs fully offline. A network connection is only used if you open this policy page or the support page from the live web instead of the copy bundled with the app.

Children

The game contains no real money gambling, no purchases and no social features. In-game credits have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or transferred.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is no remote data to export or delete. You control the local record through Android app storage settings and the in-game reset option.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the Google Play store listing for Block Balance.
